Web3 security is a whole new world where we should re-learn and change our
perspective on AppSec. In this session, I will introduce Decentralised Apps
(dApp) from security angle. I will then go under the hood of a dApp (Solidity)
vulnerability and reverse engineer a security vulnerability. I will conclude
with ways to effectively eliminate the vulnerability.

You can watch the talk on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0dcml2Sjx8).
